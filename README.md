
# FinSight Financial Analytics
### Financial Performance Analysis Using Power BI (2024–2025)

## Project Overview
FinSight Financial Analytics is a financial data analysis project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The project examines an organisation's financial performance over 2024 and 2025, with a focus on understanding why significant revenue growth did not translate into improved profitability.

The project transforms financial transaction data into interactive dashboards and management insights to support better financial decision-making.

## Business Problem
Revenue increased significantly between 2024 and 2025, but profitability deteriorated.

**Key business question:**

Why did the organisation experience substantial revenue growth without a corresponding improvement in profit?

## Project Objectives
- Analyse revenue and cost of sales.
- Evaluate gross profit and gross profit margin.
- Examine operating expenses and their major cost drivers.
- Compare operating profit and profit before tax across 2024 and 2025.
- Develop actionable recommendations for management.

## Tools and Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data modelling and star schema
- Data visualisation and financial reporting

## Key Financial Findings

| Financial Metric | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue | ₦1.41bn | ₦2.15bn |
| Gross Profit | ₦235.97m | ₦251.09m |
| Operating Expenses | ₦888.65m | ₦1.58bn |
| Operating Loss | ₦652.68m | ₦1.33bn |
| Profit Before Tax | ₦691.10m loss | ₦1.38bn loss |

### Major Insights
1. Revenue increased by approximately **52.63%**.
2. Cost of sales increased faster than revenue, by approximately **61.92%**.
3. Gross profit increased by only **6.41%**.
4. Operating expenses increased by approximately **78.04%**.
5. The operating loss and loss before tax widened considerably in 2025.

## Strategic Recommendations
1. **Protect gross profit margins:** Review supplier pricing, purchasing costs, product margins and pricing strategies.
2. **Control operating expenses:** Investigate occupancy, personnel, and selling and distribution costs.
3. **Strengthen financial monitoring:** Use monthly Power BI reports to track performance, identify cost increases and support timely management decisions.

## Power BI Report Pages

## Expected Business Value
The analysis provides management with visibility into financial performance, highlights areas of cost pressure, and supports evidence-based decisions aimed at improving profitability.
